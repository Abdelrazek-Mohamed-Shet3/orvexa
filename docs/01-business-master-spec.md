## Core Product Workflow (Authoritative Reference)

`Incoming Customer Request -> Structured Interpretation -> Product Matching -> Business Validation -> Human Review -> Human Approval or Rejection -> Order Tracking -> Operational Insights`

## Non-Negotiable Business Rules (Section 14 Summary)

1. AI-generated information must be treated as a candidate interpretation until reviewed and validated.
2. AI confidence must not be represented as a guarantee of correctness.
3. Product matching suggestions must remain distinguishable from confirmed product selections.
4. Missing or ambiguous information must be visible to the user.
5. An order must not become approved solely because AI extraction succeeded.
6. Only authorized people may approve or reject orders.
7. The original customer request should remain available for appropriate review.
8. Important edits and approval decisions should be traceable.
9. A business must not be able to view another organization's private business records.
10. Customer-specific information must be handled according to applicable privacy and retention requirements.
11. Official prices and commercial terms must follow the organization's approved business information and rules.
12. AI must not independently make financial commitments on behalf of the business.
13. AI must not independently send a final order confirmation to a customer unless explicitly authorized in a future release.
14. Unresolved validation failures must not be silently ignored.
15. The product must clearly distinguish a draft, a reviewed candidate, and an approved order.
